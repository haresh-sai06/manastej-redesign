## Title
An online clinic. Walk out with a prescription.

## Lead
Video call a therapist, talk through what is weighing on you, and leave with a clear digital prescription. The same feeling as walking out of a real doctor’s office with a plan in your hand.

## Video captions (for mute autoplay)
Hero: A calm session that ends with clarity, not an empty call.
Proof: Your homework, habits, and next visit date, ready to keep.

## Two problems

### Saves the therapist’s time
After most calls, therapists lose twenty minutes typing what just happened. Manastej listens in the background and drafts the notes automatically, so the therapist can stay with you instead of the keyboard.

### Gives you real proof and a plan
In a clinic you leave with medicines, exercises, and a next visit date on paper. Online, calls often end and the advice fades. Here you get an official digital prescription with homework, habits to follow, and when to come back.

## Feature stations

1. Book a therapist  
Pick a counselor, choose a time, and start a private video or voice session.

2. Talk to someone now  
Having a hard night? One tap connects you to a trained listener without waiting for an appointment.

3. Live session transcripts  
While you talk, the conversation becomes text on screen. Nobody has to scramble for notes.

4. Automatic doctor’s notes  
When the call ends, the therapist gets a clean one-page summary of what you discussed, how you seemed, and what was advised.

5. The digital prescription  
Your phone holds the clinic paper. A short summary, daily homework such as a few minutes of breathing or a walk, next steps, and a PDF you can download or print anytime.

6. Daily mood and sleep check-in  
Thirty seconds each day. Tap how you slept, how your energy feels, and whether stress is rising.

7. Quick stress relievers  
One-minute calming audio and breathing guides on your phone when overwhelm hits.

8. Privacy and data safety  
You decide what stays private between you and your therapist. Phone numbers and emails are hidden automatically for safety.

## Closing line
Care that listens in the room, and leaves you with something you can hold onto after.
